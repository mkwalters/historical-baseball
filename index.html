    Franchise Legends

    The five most valuable players in club history, by an era-adjusted estimate of the wins they were worth.

    How are these ranked?

    “Legend value” is a WAR-style estimate built from every season each player spent with this franchise. Hitters earn batting runs above an average hitter that season (walks, singles, doubles, triples and homers, each weighted), plus a position adjustment and credit for playing time. Pitchers earn credit for runs allowed per nine innings against that season's league. Everything is scaled to its own era, so a 1908 ace and a 2008 ace compete fairly. It ignores defense and ballparks, so treat it as a parlor estimate, not official WAR.
